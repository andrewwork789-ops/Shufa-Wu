import warnings
from statsmodels.tools.sm_exceptions import PerfectSeparationWarning


# YEAR was excluded as a predictor and used for clustering
# # PERWT is used as sampling weights, not as a predictor
variables = [
    'C(Marital_Status)', 'C(Degree)', 'C(Employement_Status)',
    'C(Generation_Category)', 'age_centered', 'age_c2',
    'adjusted_INCTOT', 'fixed_rate_30', 'adjusted_MSPUS'
]

selected = []
remaining = variables.copy()
aic_trace = []
current_aic = float('inf')
best_model = None

def fit_glm(predictors):
    formula = 'Ownership_Status ~ ' + ' + '.join(predictors)
    return smf.glm(
        formula=formula,
        data=trainData,
        family=sm.families.Binomial(),
        var_weights=trainData['PERWT_norm']
    ).fit()

while remaining:
    results = []

    for var in remaining:
        model = fit_glm(selected + [var])
        results.append((model.aic, var, model))

    best_aic, best_var, best_fit = min(results, key=lambda x: x[0])

    if best_aic >= current_aic:
        break

    selected.append(best_var)
    remaining.remove(best_var)
    current_aic = best_aic
    best_model = best_fit
    aic_trace.append((len(selected), best_aic, best_var))

    print(f"Step {len(selected):2d}: + '{best_var:<35s}'  AIC = {best_aic:.2f}")

Statistical analysis and hypothesis testing¶

Marital Status

If the Marital_Status is Never married, the log odds of owning a home decreases by 0.8127 as compared to when the individual is married and the odds of owning a home change by a factor of exp(-0.8127) = 0.44 as compared to when the individual is married, holding all other variables constant.

If the Marital_Status is Separated/Divorced, the log odds of owning a home decreases by 1.1164 as compared to when the individual is married and the odds of owning a home change by a factor of exp(-1.1164) = 0.33 as compared to when the individual is married, holding all other variables constant.

If the Marital_Status is Widowed, the log odds of owning a home decreases by 0.8911 as compared to when the individual is married and the odds of owning a home change by a factor of exp(-0.8911) = 0.41 as compared to when the individual is married, holding all other variables constant.

Null hypothesis: H0: Marital Status=0

Alternative hypothesis: HA: Marital Status!=0

Since the p-values for all Marital_Status categories are less than 0.05, we reject the null hypothesis that marital status has no effect on homeownership. This indicates that marital status has a statistically significant effect on homeownership.

Income

For a one-unit increase in adjusted_INCTOT, the log odds of owning a home increases by 4.614e-06 and the odds of owning a home increase by exp(4.614e-06) ≈ 1.000006, holding all other variables constant.

Null hypothesis: H0: Income=0

Alternative hypothesis: HA: Income!=0

Since the p-value for INCTOT is less than 0.05, we reject the null hypothesis that income has no effect on homeownership. This suggests that income is a statistically significant predictor of homeownership.

Education

If the Degree is No college Degree, the log odds of owning a home decreases by 0.1895 as compared to when the individual has a bachelor’s degree (baseline) and the odds of owning a home change by a factor of exp(-0.1895) = 0.83 as compared to the baseline, holding all other variables constant.

If the Degree is Degrees beyond a bachelor’s degree, the log odds of owning a home decreases by 0.0003 as compared to the baseline and the odds of owning a home change by a factor of exp(-0.0003) ≈ 1.00 as compared to the baseline, holding all other variables constant.

Null hypothesis: H0: Education=0

Alternative hypothesis: HA: Education!=0

Since the p-value for individuals with no college degree is less than 0.05, we reject the null hypothesis that education has no effect on homeownership. This indicates that lacking a college degree significantly affects homeownership.

Employment Status

If the Employment_Status is Unemployed, the log odds of owning a home decreases by 0.0826 as compared to when the individual is employed and the odds of owning a home change by a factor of exp(-0.0826) = 0.92 as compared to when the individual is employed, holding all other variables constant.

If the Employment_Status is Not in labor force, the log odds of owning a home increases by 0.0241 as compared to when the individual is employed and the odds of owning a home change by a factor of exp(0.0241) = 1.02 as compared to when the individual is employed, holding all other variables constant.

Null hypothesis: H0: Employment Status=0

Alternative hypothesis: HA: Employment Status!=0
